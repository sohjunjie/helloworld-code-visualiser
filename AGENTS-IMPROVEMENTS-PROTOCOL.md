# Agents Improvement Protocol

This protocol defines the continuous improvement protocol for coding agents.

---

## 1. Continuous Improvement Framework & Feedback Loop

Apply this sequential feedback loop upon completion of every task:

1. **Log Observed Anomalies & Execution Drift**
  - Reflect on the mistakes made and anomalies observed. Record them in the *Post-Mortem & Self-Reflection* section of `AGENTS-LEARNING.md`

2. **Agent Learning & Knowledge Persistence**
  - Translate your reflection into operational best practices. Record them in the *Operational Lessons & Best Practices* section of `AGENTS-LEARNING.md`
